The CARAVEL project is looking for a motivated PhD student to join our team at EURECOM and work on trustworthy AI methods for the analysis of the brain vessel tree.

The successful candidate will contribute to developing new computational methods to extract, model and analyse cerebrovascular structures from medical images, working closely with Prof. Maria A. Zuluaga and the rest of the CARAVEL team.

**What we are looking for:**
- A Master's degree in Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, Applied Mathematics or a related field
- Strong programming skills (Python) and an interest in medical image analysis and/or graph-based representations
- Good written and spoken English

Interested candidates should get in touch via the contact details on this website, including a CV and a brief statement of interest.
